**Early preview.** Tallshot is being tested by its author; expect rough edges.

Tallshot turns any scrolling window into one tall screenshot with a single hotkey: point at it, press **⌃⌥⇧⌘T**, and it scrolls to the bottom, stitches the strips, saves a PNG to your Desktop and copies it to your clipboard. No box to draw, no editor.

**What's in it**

- Capture the scrolling area under the pointer, from where you are to the bottom; or Capture from Top
- Sticky headers, chat input boxes and fixed sidebars appear once
- Esc, a click, a key or moving the mouse stops it and keeps what was captured
- Toast with the height; click to show the file in Finder
- `tallshot://capture` and `tallshot://capture?from=top` for Raycast, Shortcuts and scripts
- Settings: hotkeys, save folder, copy/save/toast, scroll speed, height and time limits, open at login
- First-run setup with pictures of exactly where to click, and a built-in self-test

**Privacy:** no network code, no third-party code, no analytics. Frames stay in memory; only the finished PNG is saved, where you chose. Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

**Install:** open the .dmg, drag Tallshot to Applications, open it, and follow the two setup steps.
